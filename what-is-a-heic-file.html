What Is a HEIC File?

If you have ever sent a photo from your iPhone to a Windows computer and found that it would not open, you have met the HEIC format. This guide explains what a HEIC file is, why your iPhone uses it, and what you can do when it causes problems.

HEIC in plain words

HEIC stands for High Efficiency Image Container. It is a way of storing photos that keeps good picture quality while using less storage space than older formats such as JPG. The files usually end in .heic, and sometimes in .heif.

HEIC is a version of a wider standard called HEIF (High Efficiency Image File Format). In everyday use, people often treat the two names as the same thing.

Why does my iPhone use HEIC?

Apple switched iPhones and iPads to HEIC with iOS 11 in 2017. The main reason is storage. At similar quality, a HEIC photo is often around half the size of a JPG, so you can keep many more pictures on the same device and in iCloud.

What can HEIC store?

  • Better color depth: HEIC can hold more color information than the 8-bit color of a standard JPG.
  • Extra data: It can store things like depth information used for Portrait mode, and image sequences such as Live Photos.
  • Edits without damage: Some editing information can be kept separately from the original image.

Why is HEIC sometimes a problem?

JPG has been around for decades and works almost everywhere. HEIC is newer, so some software, older devices, websites and printing services do not support it. You may see errors when you try to:

  • Upload a photo to a website or job application form.
  • Open pictures on an older Windows PC or a phone without HEIC support.
  • Edit photos in an app that only accepts JPG or PNG.

How to fix it

You have three simple options:

  1. Convert the photo to JPG. This is the quickest fix and works for any device. You can use our free converter below. It runs in your browser, so your photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  2. Change your iPhone camera setting. Go to Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be saved as JPG. Older photos stay as HEIC.
  3. Install support on your computer. Some systems can open HEIC after you add the right extension. See our guides for Windows and Android.

Is HEIC better than JPG?

For storage and quality, HEIC is better. For compatibility, JPG is better. Which one you choose depends on what you need to do with the photo. Read our comparison of HEIC vs JPG for details.
