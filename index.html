What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format on iPhones and iPads. It keeps good quality at a smaller file size, but many Windows PCs, Android phones, websites and apps cannot open it. Converting HEIC to JPG makes your photos work almost everywhere.

FAQ

Is this HEIC to JPG converter free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up and no limits on the number of photos.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion happens in your browser on your own device.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Will the quality drop?

At 90% quality or above, the difference is very hard to notice. Use 100% for the best quality.

Why does my iPhone save photos as HEIC?

HEIC saves storage space. You can change this in iPhone Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.
