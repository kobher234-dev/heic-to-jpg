Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use this website (the "Site") and the HEIC to JPG converter (the "Service"). By using the Site you agree to this policy.

Your photos stay on your device

The converter works entirely inside your web browser. When you select a HEIC file, it is read and converted on your own device. Your photos are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see, store or share them.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number through the converter. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, and we use it only to reply to you.

Log and technical data

Like most websites, the servers that host the Site may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of the visit. This data is used for security, performance and basic site statistics.

Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies, and we may show ads served by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies allows it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also control or delete cookies in your browser settings.

We do not control the cookies that third-party advertisers set. Please review their privacy policies for details.

Third-party services

The Site loads an open-source image conversion library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs). When the library loads, that service may receive standard technical data such as your IP address. The Site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may also keep standard server logs.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, and to object to certain processing. Because we collect very little personal data, there is usually little to access or delete, but you may contact us at any time with a request.

Security

We use HTTPS to protect the connection between your browser and the Site. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but because your photos never leave your device, they are not exposed to our servers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please email heictojpg.support@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
