How to Open HEIC Files on Windows

You copy photos from your iPhone to your Windows computer and double-click one, but it will not open, or you see a blank thumbnail. This happens because the photos are in the HEIC format. Here is how to deal with it.

Why Windows may not open HEIC

Windows needs a codec, which is a small piece of software that understands the HEIC format. Some computers already have it, and others do not. Whether HEIC works out of the box depends on your version of Windows and which updates are installed.

Option 1: Convert the photos to JPG (easiest)

Converting is often simpler than installing anything, and the JPG files will open on almost any device and website.

  1. Open our HEIC to JPG converter in your browser.
  2. Select your HEIC files. You can choose many at once.
  3. Click Convert to JPG and download the results.

Option 2: Add HEIC support to Windows

Microsoft offers codec extensions in the Microsoft Store that let Windows read HEIC photos.

  1. Open the Microsoft Store on your PC.
  2. Search for HEIF Image Extensions and install it.
  3. If your photos still do not open, search for HEVC Video Extensions as well, because HEIC photos use the HEVC codec.
  4. Restart the Photos app or your computer and try again.

Prices and availability of these extensions can change, and some versions of the HEVC extension may be paid. Check the Store page before you install.

Option 3: Change the setting on your iPhone

To avoid the problem in the future, set your iPhone to send photos in a compatible format:

  • Go to Settings > Camera > Formats and choose Most Compatible.
  • Or go to Settings > Photos, and under Transfer to Mac or PC choose Automatic.

Troubleshooting

  • Thumbnails are blank: The extension may not be installed yet, or Windows needs a restart.
  • Some HEIC files open and some do not: Files created with different settings may need the HEVC extension too.
  • You need to upload the photo to a website: Converting to JPG is the safest choice.
