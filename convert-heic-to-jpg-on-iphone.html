How to Convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone

iPhone photos are saved as HEIC by default. That is great for saving space, but it can cause trouble when you need to send or upload a picture somewhere that only accepts JPG. Here are the easiest ways to get JPG files from your iPhone.

Method 1: Use an online converter (fastest)

  1. Open our HEIC to JPG converter in Safari.
  2. Tap the upload box and choose your photos from the Photos library or the Files app.
  3. Pick the JPG quality you want. A setting around 90% is a good balance of quality and size.
  4. Tap Convert to JPG.
  5. Tap Download and save the JPG to your device.

The conversion happens inside your browser, so your photos are not sent to a server. You can select many photos at once.

Method 2: Take new photos as JPG

If you do not want HEIC at all, change the camera format:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Camera, then Formats.
  3. Choose Most Compatible.

From now on, new photos will be saved as JPG. Photos you took earlier stay in HEIC. Keep in mind that JPG files are usually larger, so they take more space on your phone.

Method 3: Let your iPhone convert when you transfer

When you connect your iPhone to a computer, you can ask it to convert photos automatically:

  1. Open Settings and tap Photos.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and find Transfer to Mac or PC.
  3. Select Automatic.

With this option, the iPhone converts photos to a compatible format when needed during the transfer.

Tips for the best result

  • Keep your original HEIC photos. They take less space and keep more detail.
  • Use a higher quality setting when you plan to edit or print the photo.
  • Use a lower quality setting if you only need a small file for email or a form.

Frequently asked questions

Will converting reduce quality? JPG compresses images, so there is a small change. At high quality settings, the difference is very hard to see.

Can I convert many photos at once? Yes. Select several files together in the converter.

Are my photos safe? With our tool, files stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
