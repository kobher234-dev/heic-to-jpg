How to Open HEIC Files on Android

A friend sends you a photo from an iPhone and your Android phone shows a broken image or says the file type is not supported. The photo is probably a HEIC file. Here is how to open it.

Does Android support HEIC?

Android added support for the HEIF/HEIC image format starting with Android 10. On newer phones, the Gallery or Google Photos app usually shows HEIC photos without any extra steps. On older versions of Android, or in some apps, the photo may not display.

Option 1: Open it in Google Photos

  1. Install or open the Google Photos app.
  2. Save the HEIC file to your phone.
  3. Open the app and look for the picture, or open it through the Files app and choose Google Photos.

Option 2: Convert it to JPG

If the photo will not open, or you need to share it with an app that rejects HEIC, convert it to JPG:

  1. Open our HEIC to JPG converter in Chrome.
  2. Tap the upload box. If you cannot see your HEIC file, browse through the Files or Downloads folder instead of the photo gallery.
  3. Tap Convert to JPG, then Download.

Your photos are converted in your browser and are not uploaded to a server.

Ask the sender for a different format

If you often receive iPhone photos, ask the sender to change their camera format. On the iPhone this is under Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible. New photos will then be JPG and will open anywhere.

Tips

  • If a photo was sent as a file or document, it keeps its HEIC format. Photos sent through some chat apps are converted automatically.
  • A HEIC file may not appear in the gallery on some phones. Look for it in the Downloads folder with the Files app.
  • Keep your phone's software up to date for the best format support.
