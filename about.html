About HEIC to JPG

Who we are

HEIC to JPG is a free online tool that helps people open and share photos taken on iPhones and iPads. Modern iPhones save pictures in the HEIC format, which many Windows PCs, Android phones, websites and apps cannot open. Our converter turns those photos into standard JPG files that work almost everywhere.

Our mission

We believe a simple task should stay simple. You should be able to convert a photo in a few taps, without creating an account, installing software, or handing your pictures to a stranger's server. That is why we built this tool to be fast, free and private.

What makes us different

  • Private by design: The conversion runs inside your own browser. Your photos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Free to use: No sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of photos.
  • Batch conversion: Select many HEIC files at once and convert them in one go.
  • Adjustable quality: Choose the JPG quality that suits you, from smaller files to maximum quality.
  • Works on any device: Use it on a phone, tablet or computer with a modern web browser.

How we keep the site free

This website may display advertising to cover hosting and development costs. Ads never change how the converter works, and we do not have access to your photos.
